### UI Design & Theme Guidelines (Based on "image_a09784.png")

**1. Brand Color Palette:**
*   **Primary (Deep Midnight Blue):** `#0B2545` (Derived from the top of the TC logo) - Used for primary headers, navigation bars, and dominant structural elements.
*   **Accent / Highlight (Sunset Orange / Coral Gradient):** `#E07A5F` to `#F4A261` (Derived from the logo's gradient base) - Used sparingly for call-to-actions (CTAs), active states, alerts, and critical data highlights.
*   **Neutral Dark:** `#131921` (For dark mode backgrounds)
*   **Neutral Light:** `#F8F9FA` or `#EDF2F4` (For light mode backgrounds and clean card containers)

**2. Visual Style & Typography:**
*   **Typography:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Montserrat) to match the bold, modern lettering of "TRAFFICAM" in image_a09784.png.
*   **Component Styling:** Modern, semi-flat design with subtle border radiuses (8px–12px) for cards and containers. Use soft drop shadows to create depth without cluttering the data-heavy layout.
*   **Interface Elements:** Incorporate sleek, high-contrast data visualizations (charts, maps, and status badges). Map lines or track paths should utilize the accent orange gradient against a dark or muted canvas for maximum legibility.

**3. Theme Configurations:**
*   *Dark Mode (Recommended default):* A premium, midnight-blue dashboard vibe. Deep dark backgrounds with glowing orange accent telemetry and crisp white text.
*   *Light Mode:* Crisp white and light gray backgrounds, utilizing the midnight blue for typography and structured containers, with the orange gradient reserved for buttons and key metrics.
